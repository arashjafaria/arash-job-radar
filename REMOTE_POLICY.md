# Remote work and geography — clarified 25 November 2026

Inside the existing allowed city/radius scope, onsite, hybrid, Homeoffice and Mobiles Arbeiten remain eligible for the other job filters. Outside it (including a generic Germany location), only an explicit FULL remote offer can waive the geographic restriction: fully remote, 100% remote, vollständig remote, komplett im Homeoffice, or equivalent wording. Plain Remote, Homeoffice, mobile work by agreement and partial/hybrid work do not suffice. Regular mandatory office attendance or negated full-remote wording prevents this override.

This clarifies and supersedes the broader 23 November policy. Matching, second review, final delivery and ALTEN feed-title checks share the same full-remote rule. Discovery sources and queries, onsite scope, all other filters and duplicate protection are retained. Old notification history must not be deleted or reset; previously sent jobs are not reposted.
